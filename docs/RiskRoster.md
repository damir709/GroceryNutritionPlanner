# Risk Register

## Purpose

If there are any risks that could occur with this project, they will be documented below.

## Risk Assessment Scale

### Probability
- Low (L): Small chance to happen, quite unlikely
- Medium (M): Moderate chance of happening
- High (H): Very likely to happen

### Impact
- Low (L): Small effect on the project
- Medium (M): Fairly noticeable effect on the project as a whole
- High (H): Very large impact on the project, needing immediate action

### Risk Status Definitions

- Open: Risk is currently on-going
- Handled: Risk was dealt with, project affected
- Closed: Risk is not a problem anymore, project unaffected
